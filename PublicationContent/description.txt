[h1]CustomWeapon (CWP)[/h1]
Custom weapon package for [b]Killing Floor 2[/b] with adjusted weapon stats, custom damage types, alternate fire modes, weapon skins, and balance changes for regular and AT variants.

[b]Mutator:[/b]
[code]CWP.CWPMut[/code]

[h1]Recent Balance Updates[/h1]

[h2]Deagle[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Deagle Ex / Dual Deagle Ex[/b] — Vanilla recoil restored: 650 max / 550 min. Fire rate matches vanilla: 300 RPM single / approximately 545 RPM dual.
[*][b]Tactical reload[/b] — 20% faster while rounds remain in the magazine.
[*][b]Evil Deagle[/b] — 115 damage, 350 RPM, 12-round magazine, 132 reserve rounds, reduced recoil.
[*][b]Dual Evil's Deagle[/b] — 115 damage per bullet, 650 RPM, 24-round magazine, 264 reserve rounds, reduced recoil.
[*][b]AT variants[/b] — Right-click toggles full-auto mode while retaining the matching Deagle fire rate and recoil.
[/list]

[h2]Support Shotguns[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]AA-12 Ex[/b] — 168 total damage per shot (+20%), reload 15% faster and 30% faster while rounds remain.
[*][b]Double-Barreled Boomstick Ex[/b] — 275 single / 550 double total damage (+10%), reload 25% faster and 45% faster while rounds remain.
[*][b]Elephant Gun Ex[/b] — 264 total damage per shot (+10%), reload 25% faster and 45% faster while rounds remain.
[*][b]HZ12 Multi-Action Ex[/b] — 240 total damage per shot (+20%), reload 15% faster and 30% faster while rounds remain.
[*][b]M4 Combat Shotgun Ex[/b] — 276 total damage per shot (+15%), reload 15% faster.
[*][b]S12 Shockgun Ex[/b] — 235.2 shotgun damage (+20%), 240 alt-fire explosion damage (+20%), reload 15% faster and 30% faster while rounds remain.
[/list]

[h2]Other Recent Balance Changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]M1911 Ex / Dual M1911 Ex[/b] — Vanilla recoil restored: 450 max / 400 min. AT variants inherit the same recoil.
[*][b]Glock 18C Ex[/b] — Inventory weight reduced to 3.
[*][b]Dual Glock 18C Ex[/b] — Inventory weight reduced to 6.
[*][b]Magnum / SW500[/b] — Damage 180 and stumble power reduced to 40. Dual reload is 45% faster.
[*][b]M14 variants[/b] — ACOG and No Scope use 90 damage; RS uses 116 damage.
[*][b]AF2011 Ex AT[/b] — Corrected shop name; displayed as [b]AF2011 Ex AT[/b].
[*][b]HRG Crossbow Ex[/b] — Distinguished from the regular Crossbow Ex in the shop as [b]HRG Crossbow Ex[/b].
[/list]

[h1]SkinsAdder Exceptions[/h1]

The following custom weapons intentionally do not use vanilla skin replacement:

[list]
[*]AutoMag Ex and Dual AutoMag Ex
[*]M107 Ex
[*]M110 SASS Ex
[*]M24 Ex
[*]Casull and Jackal & Casull
[*]Syringe Armor
[/list]

Evil Deagle single and dual variants use the Deagle skin mapping. M14 variants remain mapped to the vanilla M14 skin.

[h1]Notes[/h1]

[list]
[*]Damage values for shotguns are shown as total damage per firing event in the descriptions; source damage is configured per pellet.
[*]Reload bonuses that mention remaining rounds use the partial/tactical reload condition.
[*]The package includes custom WeaponDefs, damage types, weapon classes, AT variants, and skin mappings.
[*]Do not reupload this package without permission.
[/list]
